Questions

Before you join.

What does it cost?

Joining the waitlist is free, and so are the six open reports. Anything we charge for at launch will be announced before it opens, and the waitlist hears first.

How do you choose the stays?

By hand. We read guest reviews, press, award lists and official data, and only include places that hold up. If we ever feature a sponsored stay, it will be clearly labelled, and it never changes a score.

How does Sana make money?

Today it doesn't: the preview is free. At launch we plan to earn a commission when you book through Sana, at no extra cost to you. Anything sponsored will always be labelled.

What will you email me?

One email to confirm your address, one when Sana opens, and now and then a note on new stays and seasonal picks. Unsubscribe any time.
